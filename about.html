Our name

Nasihah نصيحة

naṣīḥah · noun · Arabic

Sincere counsel — advice given honestly, in the genuine interest of the person who receives it.

Nasihah is more than a name — it is the way we practise. Integrity, sincerity and compassion sit behind every file: advice grounded in honesty, clients who feel heard and respected, and solutions built around what a person actually needs.

So when you ask us what will happen, you get our real assessment: what the law says, what the evidence supports, what it is likely to cost and what we think you should do. If a case is weak, we say so. If a dispute is better settled than fought, we say that too.

How we work

From first enquiry to resolution

Every matter is different, but the path is usually the same four steps — and you’ll always know which one you’re on.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Call, email or send the enquiry form with a brief outline of your matter and any court or tribunal dates.

  2. 02

    Consult

    We meet in person, by phone or by video, go through your documents and tell you where you stand.

  3. 03

    Plan

    You receive our advice, a costs disclosure and an agreed plan before further work is carried out.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    We negotiate, mediate or run the matter in court or at VCAT — and keep you updated as it moves.

Access

Advice you can actually follow

Legal problems are hard enough without a language barrier or a wall of jargon. If English isn’t your first language, say so when you get in touch and we will arrange an accredited interpreter for your appointment and for court.

Appointments can be in person at Coburg, by telephone or by video, and documents can be signed electronically in most matters — so distance, work hours and caring responsibilities don’t stop you getting advice.

Costs & communication

What our fees look like

Before we start work, you receive a costs disclosure setting out how you will be charged, our estimate for each stage and your rights about costs. Nothing beyond that estimate happens without your instructions.

  • Fixed fees for work that can be scoped up front, such as conveyancing, a simple will or a single court appearance.
  • Hourly rates for matters that can’t be predicted in advance, with stage estimates and regular updates as costs accrue.
  • No surprises — if a matter changes direction and the estimate needs to change, we tell you before the extra work is done.
A note on first appointments. Please bring anything you have: letters, contracts, court documents, photographs, texts and emails. Even a rough chronology of dates helps us give you useful advice on the day.

Talk to us about your matter

Tell us what’s happened and what you’d like to achieve. We’ll explain your options, the likely steps and what they cost — before you commit to anything.
