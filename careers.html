Careers

A small firm where the work is varied, the responsibility is real and the standard is set by how carefully things are done — not by how many hours are recorded.

Expressions of interest are welcome at any time, whether or not a role is advertised.

Email us

Why here

Close to the work, close to the client

In a firm this size there is nowhere to hide and no file you only see a corner of. You will draft, you will speak to clients, and you will go to court — with supervision, and with the time to do the work properly.

We act for people in the middle of difficult moments, often in their second or third language. The people who do well here are patient, precise and straight with clients about bad news as well as good.

What we look for

Care, clarity and follow-through

Care with detail

Dates, evidence and drafting. Most matters turn on the things other people skim past.

Plain speaking

The ability to explain a legal position to someone who has never seen a courtroom, without condescension.

Ownership

Files move because someone moves them. We look for people who close loops without being chased.

Current opportunities

We are not advertising a specific role at the moment. Expressions of interest are still welcome — send your CV and we will keep it on file and come back to you when something opens up.

Roles we typically recruit

Solicitor

Running your own files across one or more of our practice areas, with supervision and appearance work from the start. Suited to lawyers with post-admission experience in litigation, family law or property who want breadth rather than a narrow specialisation.

Law clerk / paralegal

Supporting file management, drafting correspondence, preparing court documents and briefs, and dealing with clients day to day. A good fit for penultimate and final-year law students who want genuine responsibility while they study.

Administration and reception

Often the first voice a client hears. Diary and file management, billing support, and keeping the office running. Organised, warm and unflappable is the brief.

Seasonal clerkship and work experience

A limited number of short placements for students who want to see how a small general practice actually works. Tell us what you want to learn and when you are available.

How to apply

  1. Email us at info@nasihahlegal.com.au with “Expression of interest” or the role name in the subject line.
  2. Attach a CV and a short cover email — half a page is plenty. Tell us which areas of law interest you and why.
  3. Include your details: admission date (if admitted), languages spoken, and your availability.
  4. We reply to everyone. If we are not recruiting, we’ll say so rather than leaving you waiting.
A note for students. If you are looking for supervised practical experience or a placement that counts toward your course requirements, say so in your email and include the requirements — it helps us tell you quickly whether we can accommodate it.
