Privacy policy & terms of use

How we handle personal information, and the terms on which this website is provided.

Last updated: 23 September 2026
Questions: info@nasihahlegal.com.au

Privacy policy

Nasihah Legal (“we”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, as well as our professional obligations of confidentiality.

What we collect

We collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide legal services and to run our practice, which may include:

  • your name, contact details and identification documents;
  • information about your matter, including documents and correspondence you give us;
  • financial information needed for billing, trust accounting and settlement;
  • information about other people relevant to your matter, where you provide it; and
  • enquiry details you submit through this website.

Where the law requires it — including identity verification for property transactions — we may be obliged to collect and retain particular documents.

How we collect it

Usually directly from you: in a meeting, by telephone, by email, or through the enquiry form on this website. Sometimes we collect information from third parties, such as another party’s lawyer, a court or tribunal, a government register, an expert, or an agent acting for you.

How we use it

We use personal information to provide legal services and advice, to communicate with you, to check for conflicts of interest, to bill and collect payment, to comply with our legal and professional obligations, and to improve our services.

When we disclose it

We disclose personal information only where necessary — for example to a court or tribunal, to another party or their lawyer in the conduct of your matter, to barristers and experts we brief on your behalf, to government agencies where required, and to service providers who assist us (such as IT and document services) under confidentiality obligations. We do not sell personal information. Some of the service providers we use may store information on servers outside Australia. Where that is the case, we take reasonable steps to ensure the information is handled in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Confidentiality and legal professional privilege

Information you give us for the purpose of obtaining legal advice is confidential and is generally protected by legal professional privilege. That protection belongs to you, and we will not waive it without your instructions except where the law requires disclosure.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including access controls, secure file storage and staff confidentiality obligations. We retain files for the period required by our professional obligations — generally at least seven years after a matter ends — and then destroy them securely.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Write to info@nasihahlegal.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable time. In limited circumstances we may refuse access — for example where doing so would breach another person’s privacy or legal professional privilege — and we will explain why.

This website

Submitting the enquiry form sends the information you enter to us by email through a third-party form service. Please don’t include sensitive or confidential details in that first message. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track visitors across other sites.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first at info@nasihahlegal.com.au and we will investigate. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. Complaints about our legal services can also be made to the Victorian Legal Services Board and Commissioner.

  • No lawyer–client relationship. Using this website, or sending an enquiry through it, does not create a lawyer–client relationship. That relationship begins only when we have checked for conflicts, accepted your instructions and provided a costs disclosure and engagement letter.
