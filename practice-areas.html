Practice areas

Five areas of law, one point of contact. If your problem spans more than one — and many do — you won’t be handed between firms.

Not sure which area covers your situation? Describe it in a few sentences and we’ll tell you.

Ask us
I.

Civil Litigation

From a disputed invoice to a contested trial: advice on the merits, then negotiation, mediation or proceedings in the Magistrates’, County or Supreme Court, or at VCAT.

  • Contract disputes and debt recovery
  • Building and construction disputes
  • Motor vehicle accident claims
  • Consumer law and employment disputes
  • Magistrates’, County and Supreme Court · VCAT
Learn more
II.

Criminal Law

Charged, summonsed or under investigation? We explain the charges, the evidence and the realistic outcomes, then prepare your matter properly for court.

  • Assault charges and family violence matters
  • Drug, theft and sexual offences
  • Firearms and weapons offences
  • Traffic offences, pleas, contests and diversion
Learn more
III.

Family Law

Separation is difficult enough. We focus on practical arrangements for children and a fair division of property, with court as the last resort rather than the first.

  • Divorce, separation and consent orders
  • Children’s living and time arrangements
  • Property and financial settlements
  • Binding Financial Agreements, mediation and FDR
Learn more
IV.

Wills, Estates & Part IV

Putting your affairs in order, administering an estate, or bringing or defending a family provision claim under Part IV of the Administration and Probate Act 1958 (Vic).

  • Wills and estate planning documents
  • Probate and estate administration
  • Part IV family provision claims
  • Executor and beneficiary disputes
Learn more
V.

Property Law

Conveyancing handled by a lawyer, plus the property disputes that conveyancers can’t take on — from boundaries and fences to possessory title.

  • Conveyancing — purchase and sale
  • Contract and section 32 review
  • Leases, caveats and property disputes
  • Adverse possession, fencing and boundaries
Learn more

Referrals

If it’s not our field

We don’t take on work outside these areas. If your matter belongs somewhere else — migration, tax, workers compensation — we’ll tell you at the first call and point you toward someone who does it well.
