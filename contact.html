After you get in touch

What happens next

  1. 01

    We read it

    Your enquiry comes straight to us — not to a call centre. Urgent matters and imminent court dates are prioritised.

  2. 02

    We reply

    We tell you whether this is work we can take on, and if it isn’t, we point you toward someone who can.

  3. 03

    We meet

    In person in Coburg, by phone or by video. Bring your documents — even the ones you think don’t matter.

  4. 04

    You decide

    You get our advice, a costs disclosure and a plan. Nothing further happens without your say-so.
